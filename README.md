## 📂 What I have built

### 🤖 [linkedin-job-bot](https://github.com/Bhavya-Bansal-49/linkedin-job-bot)

An agent that wakes up before I do and emails me the 20 roles worth my attention.

- Scrapes PM listings by keyword and location, dedupes against what it already sent
- Adds one click networking searches for alumni and locals at each company
- Read only by design, so no auto applying and no account flags
- Python, Selenium, cron, Gmail app passwords

### 📬 [email-sanity-agent](https://github.com/Bhavya-Bansal-49/email-sanity-agent)

Classifies the morning inbox and tells me what actually needs a reply. Built after one too many mornings spent sorting instead of working.

### 🎙 [ai-meeting-notes](https://github.com/Bhavya-Bansal-49/ai-meeting-notes)

A macOS menu bar toggle that transcribes any call: Zoom, Meet, Teams, a phone on speaker. Everything runs on the laptop.

- Local transcription with faster-whisper, no cloud backend and no per vendor integration
- Audio routed through BlackHole and an aggregate device so you still hear the meeting
- Drops a plain .txt into whatever synced folder you point it at
- Deliberately does not summarize, because I wanted the raw transcript

### 🎮 [amd-diagram](https://github.com/Bhavya-Bansal-49/amd-diagram)

An interactive walkthrough of how work moves through an AMD GPU, from CPU dispatch to result writeback.

- 15 animated steps covering grids, work groups, wavefronts, CU and WGP scheduling, LDS, caches, VRAM
- Clickable architecture blocks with an inspector panel and a searchable glossary
- AMD specific on purpose: wavefronts, not warps, with CDNA and RDNA differences called out
- React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind

### 📓 [amd-prep](https://github.com/Bhavya-Bansal-49/amd-prep)

Notes and kernel experiments from teaching myself GPU compute: HIP against CUDA, Triton on MI300X, and a visual glossary of the architecture.

### ⌚ [watch-w-whoop-band](https://github.com/Bhavya-Bansal-49/watch-w-whoop-band)

A manufacturer ready design kit for wearing a watch and a Whoop on the same wrist. Three strap variants, a shared sensor pod cradle, dimensioned SVG drawings, tooling costs, and supplier RFQs. A hardware detour, and I regret nothing.
